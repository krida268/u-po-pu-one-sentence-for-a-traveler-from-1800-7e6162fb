One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800.

The entry: "Picture one road joining every library and post office on earth, so a letter crosses the sea before its ink dries and any book can be read at your own table." One road joining every library and post office on earth — letters that arrive before the ink dries, books read at your own table — built only from things a person from 1800 already knows.
